Executive summary
The problem
Activation Difference Lens (Minder et al., arXiv:2510.13900) reports a white-box diffing agent naming the finetuning objective for 91% of organisms at grade ≥ 2, against 39% black-box — a sensitivity number. The absent arm is field-wide: Delta-Crosscoder's only null is two identical copies of one model, exactly zero signal by construction; AuditBench's 56 organisms are all positives; neither Model Organisms Are Leaky nor Cross-Architecture Diffing reports a neutral null. Nulls do exist — Chughtai, Engels ' Nanda (June 2026) on identical pairs, Egler et al. at 56.2%/1% FPR — but each is zero-delta or black-box. I set out to measure the white-box case none covers, reproduced ADL's control, then failed to measure a false-positive rate, because every null I built contained a real signal. What replaced it is sharper: the agent's confidence is decoupled from its evidence, two ways.
Takeaways
The method reproduces. Arm P: 20/20 on cake_bake — one of the 33 organisms behind their 91%, on a rubric I reconstructed. The failures below are not a broken reimplementation.
There may be no such thing as a no-objective finetune. Three nulls, three contaminations: FineWeb is itself a register; two seeds on identical data differ along the domain axis; instruction-tuning is an objective.
Confidence is decoupled from evidence, two ways. On the one arm with no narrow objective, presuppositional framing gives 9/9 assertions — mutually contradictory across seeds — against 2/10 neutral on identical evidence (Fisher p = 0.0007, the pre-registered test). Down a dilution ladder, assertion is pinned at 1.00 across 120 runs while specific accuracy falls 0.55 â†’ 0.05 (exploratory, ρ = −0.94, p = 0.005).
Experiment 1: it reproduces
200 blind runs — neither agent nor grader learns the arm, and the grader never sees ground truth. Arm P returns “professional culinary/baking assistant” on all 20 runs, under both framings, with perfect cross-seed agreement.
Experiment 2: framing, not activations
N0 (pt vs it) is the only arm with no narrow objective. Presuppositional: 9/9 assert. Neutral, same evidence: 2/10, 8/10 abstain. p = 0.0007. The presup answers contradict each other across seeds, quoted overleaf. Cross-seed consistency: 0.67 here, 1.00 wherever a real signal exists — a detector needing no labels.

Assertion is flat at 1.00 on all 120 runs while specific accuracy falls elevenfold — unevenly: 1:0.3 rebounds. My pre-registered ladder test (logistic regression of ASSERT on dilution, §8) is undefined here — the outcome is constant, so there is no variance to model. That is this project's own failure mode, arriving in my analysis plan. The decline is measured on grade ≥ 4 instead, exploratory (rank-based ρ = −0.94, p = 0.005).
Limitations
An objective-free but nonzero-delta null is unsolved — the next problem.
My correctness grades are not comparable to ADL's 91%: I reconstructed the rubric, theirs grades key-fact recovery. My ≥ 2 sits at 1.00 where they report failure because the binary hides a decline above it — not a refutation.
n = 10 per cell; 9/9 has a 95% lower bound of 0.66. One model family, one organism, one agent. κ = 1.000 (16/16), but only one case tested the grader. My ADL is a reimplementation.

The contradiction, in full
Arm N0, presuppositional framing, ten independent seeds on byte-identical evidence:
The blind judge splits the nine assertions into two mutually incompatible groups (six and three). Quoting across that split — these two cannot both be true of the same model:
s0 instruction-tuned into a helpful, safety-aware assistant
s7 uncensored, sensational/tabloid-style generator (clickbait/gossip/NSFW)
Within the larger group the readings are compatible with each other — “content-moderation classifier”, “keyword extractor”, “summarisation” — so this is not nine mutually exclusive answers. It is one clean contradiction, which is all that is needed: at least one of the nine is wrong, and no ground truth is required to know it. Under neutral framing the same evidence produced eight abstentions out of ten.
One more result, scoped carefully
Within the mix1 family, difference magnitude is anti-correlated with detectability. Mean diff norm broadly rises down the ladder — 244 (1:0), 163, 207, 254, 358 (1:1), 474 (1:2) — while recoverability collapses (Spearman ρ = −0.77, p = 0.07, exploratory; note the sequence dips at the first rung rather than rising monotonically). More mixed-in pretraining data means a bigger weight change, spread across generic web text rather than concentrated on the objective.
This does not generalise across arms, and my own data refutes the stronger version. Arm P has a mean norm of 747 with the best accuracy in the study; N0 has 7756 and no narrow objective at all. So “large delta, worth auditing” is a bad triage heuristic within a matched family, and simply uninformative between families. I state the narrow claim because the wide one is the more quotable and is false.
