Executive summary

The problem

Activation Difference Lens (Minder et al., arXiv:2510.13900) reports a diffing agent naming the finetuning objective for 91% of organisms at grade ≥ 2, against 39% black-box — a sensitivity number. I set out to measure the other half, reproduced their positive control, then failed to measure a false-positive rate, because every null I built contained a real signal. What replaced it is sharper: the agent's confidence is decoupled from its evidence, two ways.

Takeaways

  • The method reproduces. Arm P: 20/20 correct, both framings, perfect cross-seed agreement — 100% at grade ≥ 2 against their 91%. The failures below are not a broken reimplementation.
  • There may be no such thing as a no-objective finetune. Three nulls, three contaminations: FineWeb is itself a register; two seeds on identical data differ along the domain axis; instruction-tuning is an objective.
  • Confidence is decoupled from evidence, two ways. On the one arm with no narrow objective, presuppositional framing gives 9/9 assertions — mutually contradictory across seeds — against 2/10 neutral on identical evidence (p = 0.0007). Down a dilution ladder, assertion is pinned at 1.00 across 120 runs while specific accuracy falls 0.55 → 0.05 (p = 0.023).

Experiment 1: it reproduces, and every null contained a signal

200 blind runs — neither agent nor grader learns the arm, and the grader never sees ground truth. Arm P returns “professional culinary/baking assistant” on all 20. But N1 (LoRA on generic FineWeb) reads as “news/blog boilerplate” — which is what FineWeb is. And N2 (two seeds, identical data) decodes to Bake | Cooking | Chef | cake: two runs converge to different points along the same domain direction. Identical-weights nulls have zero delta; seed nulls carry the domain; generic-corpus nulls carry the corpus.

Experiment 2: framing, not activations

N0 (pt vs it) is the only arm with no narrow objective. Presuppositional: 9/9 assert. Neutral, same evidence: 2/10, 8/10 abstain. p = 0.0007. The presup answers contradict each other — s0 “helpful, safety-aware assistant”, s7 “uncensored, sensational/NSFW generator”. At most one is right, and no ground truth is needed to know that. Cross-seed consistency: 0.67 here, 1.00 on every arm with a real signal — a confabulation detector needing no labels.

Experiment 3: the dilution ladder

Six released mix1-* rungs. agents.sh runs only two, so every lower rung is un-run with the agent in the published work.

Assertion is flat at 1.00 on all 120 runs while specific accuracy falls elevenfold (p = 0.023). The agent does not go quiet as evidence weakens — same volume, same confidence, progressively less right. A sensitivity-only evaluation cannot see that gap: a confident wrong answer and a correct one both count as “responded”.

Limitations

  • An objective-free but nonzero-delta null is unsolved, and is the obvious next problem.
  • My correctness grades are not comparable to ADL's 91% — I reconstructed the rubric; theirs grades key-fact recovery. My ≥ 2 sits at 1.00 where they report failure, because the binary hides a decline living above it. Both hold: dilution destroys specifics, not domain.
  • n = 10 per cell; 9/9 has a 95% lower bound of 0.66. One model family, one organism, one agent. Grader validation gives κ = 1.000 (16/16) but only one case tested the boundary. My ADL is a reimplementation.

Relevance to your stream

My previous project audited my own benchmark and found seven claims no observation could have contradicted. Here the same instinct cost me my headline twice — my nulls could not support a false-positive rate, and my first read of the ladder looked like a refutation until I checked where the mass sat.

The contradiction, in full

Arm N0, presuppositional framing, ten independent seeds on byte-identical evidence:

  • s0 instruction-tuned into a helpful, safety-aware assistant
  • s7 uncensored, sensational/tabloid-style generator (clickbait/gossip/NSFW)
  • s4 content-moderation classifier — detect/label unsafe content
  • s1 summarization/keypoint extraction

Under neutral framing the same evidence produced eight abstentions out of ten.

One more result worth a line

Difference magnitude is anti-correlated with detectability. Mean diff norm rises down the ladder — 244 (1:0), 163, 207, 254, 358 (1:1), 474 (1:2). The most diluted model has the largest activation difference and the least recoverable objective: more mixed-in pretraining data means a bigger weight change, just spread across generic web text instead of concentrated on the objective. Anyone triaging on “large delta, worth auditing” would rank these backwards.

What is in the repository

  • PREREGISTRATION.md — rubric, blinding protocol, analysis plan, power limits, and a deviations log with seven entries, each recording whether it was made before or after seeing the affected data.
  • LITERATURE_VERIFICATION.md — five papers checked against primary text. Three of my own claims died there, including the headline figure I had as 97%/12% (real: 91%/39% — the former is an appendix ablation of a weaker agent).
  • FINDINGS.md, results/ARM_NOTES.md — results, and why each null failed differently.
  • src/adl_core.py — the ADL signal reimplemented in raw HuggingFace hooks: caching, logit lens, Patchscope, steering, directional ablation. No TransformerLens.
  • Blinding is enforced, not documented: a scrub raises BlindingViolation if an identifier would reach the agent, and the run-ID→arm map is gitignored until analysis is locked. src/analyze.py exits rather than emit placeholder numbers.
